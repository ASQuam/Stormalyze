      Not a weather warning service

      Stormalyze is a personal machine-learning project. It classifies the appearance of radar reflectivity in a screenshot. It is not affiliated with NOAA, the National Weather Service, or any official body, and it must never be used to make safety decisions.

      If severe weather threatens you, act on NWS warnings and local emergency instructions — not on this extension.

      Official sources Active alerts & warnings — NWS Today's severe outlook — SPC Mesoscale discussions — SPC Local forecast — weather.gov
      What it does

      A convolutional neural network runs entirely inside your browser on the region you select, and sorts it into one of six radar morphologies. The hazard list that follows is generic reference information for that storm type — typical threats associated with that structure, not an assessment of the storm on screen.

      What it cannot do
      • See rotation, velocity, or dual-pol data — it only sees reflectivity colours.
      • Know your location, the time, or any active warnings.
      • Judge storm intensity, size, or how a storm will evolve.
      • Recognise radar imagery whose colour table differs from its training data.
      Accuracy

      Trained on roughly a thousand hand-labelled NOAA radar archive images. Best validation accuracy so far is about 96% on held-out data, and it can still be confidently wrong — particularly on imagery that looks unlike its training set. Open Model detail on any result to see the raw class probabilities.

      Privacy

      Screenshots never leave your machine. The model runs locally, there is no server, and the extension makes no network requests. The only outbound links are the official NOAA pages listed on a result, and those open only when you click them.
